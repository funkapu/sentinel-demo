# Notely privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-01

## What we collect

When you sign up we collect your email address and your name so you can
log in and so we can contact you about your account.

The notes you write are stored in our database.

## Who we share it with

We use Stripe to take payments. Stripe receives your email address, name,
and account ID so it can process your subscription.

Note content you choose to summarize is sent to OpenAI to generate the
summary. OpenAI does not use it to train models under our API agreement.

Our database and realtime features are hosted by Supabase.

We do not sell your data.
